Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the private Instagram automation application ("Automation", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information received through Meta's Instagram APIs.

The App is used by its owner to automate Instagram comment and messaging workflows for Instagram accounts connected by the owner.

2. Information We Receive

When an Instagram account is connected to the App, the App may receive information made available through Meta's Instagram APIs, including:

  • Instagram account identifiers.
  • Instagram usernames and profile information made available by the API.
  • Instagram-scoped user identifiers.
  • Comments and comment-related information.
  • Information associated with Instagram messaging interactions.
  • Information required to determine whether an interaction can proceed according to the configured Instagram automation workflow.

3. How Information Is Used

Information received through Instagram is used only to operate the configured Instagram automation features, including:

  • Detecting comments on connected Instagram content.
  • Matching comments against configured keywords.
  • Executing configured automated responses.
  • Sending Instagram messages when permitted by Meta's APIs.
  • Managing automation rules, templates, logs, and duplicate protection.
  • Checking information required for an automation workflow.

4. Data Storage

The App may store configuration information and limited technical data required to operate the automation service, such as automation rules, keyword configurations, message templates, event identifiers, and processing logs.

Instagram information is not intentionally collected for unrelated purposes.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell Instagram user information.

Information received from Instagram may be processed by infrastructure and service providers used to operate the App, such as hosting, database, logging, and Meta's API infrastructure.

We do not intentionally disclose Instagram user information to unrelated third parties except where required to operate the App, comply with applicable law, or protect the security of the App.

6. Meta / Instagram

The App uses Meta's Instagram APIs and is subject to the applicable Meta Platform Terms, Developer Policies, and Instagram API requirements.

Instagram authentication and access are handled through Meta's authorized authentication mechanisms. The App does not request or store Instagram passwords.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by the App. Access credentials and API credentials should be stored securely and should not be publicly disclosed.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the configured automation features, maintain technical logs, or satisfy legitimate operational and legal requirements.

Users may request deletion of information associated with an Instagram integration by contacting the App owner using the contact information provided below.

9. Disconnecting Instagram

An Instagram account can be disconnected from the App by removing or revoking the applicable Instagram authorization. After disconnection, the App will no longer use the authorization to access Instagram through the applicable API connection.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality, Instagram API requirements, or applicable legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or questions about this application, contact the App owner through the contact information associated with the application.
